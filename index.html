Privacy Policy for TOO HEAVY!

Developer: DubIT Games Contact: kuba045001@gmail.com Effective date: 15 November 2026

This policy explains what the Android game TOO HEAVY! ("the game") does with information. In short: DubIT Games does not collect your personal data. The game is single-player and works offline, but it shows optional ads and sells optional purchases, and the services that provide those (Google AdMob and Google Play) handle some data, as described below.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no sign-up.
  • DubIT Games does not collect, receive, store or sell any personal information about you.
  • Your progress is saved on your device.
  • The game shows optional rewarded ads (for example "watch an ad to continue after a crash") through Google AdMob. Google collects some device information for that, described below.
  • The game sells optional in-app purchases (cosmetic looks, packs of retries, and a monthly "Double Coins" subscription). Google Play handles every payment.
  • The game has no chat, no social features and no user-generated content.

What the game stores on your device

To remember where you are, the game saves a small file of game data on your device:

  • which levels you have cleared, and how many gems you have collected;
  • your coin balance, what you have built in the Skyport, and which balloon and flame looks you own and have equipped;
  • which premium looks you have bought, and whether your Double Coins subscription was active the last time Google Play was asked (the game asks Google Play each time it starts, and when you tap "Restore purchases");
  • how many retries you have left, and the order numbers of your most recent retries purchases, kept only so that one purchase is never counted twice;
  • small settings such as sound on or off and the volume, whether you have finished the first lessons, and when the game last showed you an offer;
  • how many seconds you have spent flying, which the game uses to decide when to offer help;
  • the time of your last daily bonus, so the game can tell when a new day has started on your device's clock.

None of this identifies you. It stays on your device, and DubIT Games never receives it. You can erase it at any time by clearing the game's storage in Android settings (Settings > Apps > TOO HEAVY! > Storage > Clear data) or by uninstalling the game. If you reinstall, "Restore purchases" in the Paint Shop brings back the looks and the subscription you bought with the same Google account. Retries are used up as you play, so retries you have not used yet are lost if you clear the game's storage or uninstall it.

If Android backup is switched on for your device, Android may include the game's saved data in your own backup to your own Google account. That is controlled by you and Google through your device settings; DubIT Games cannot see it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob, an advertising service of Google, to show rewarded video ads that you choose to watch. The game needs an internet connection for ads; everything else plays offline.

  • AdMob, a third party, collects and uses information from your device to choose and show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. According to Google's own description of its ads SDK, this includes your device's advertising ID and app set ID, your IP address (from which a general location can be estimated), how you interact with the app and ads (such as app launches, taps and video views), and diagnostic information (such as app launch time and hangs). This data is sent to Google, encrypted in transit.
  • Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game shows Google's consent form (the User Messaging Platform) the first time you start the game, before any ad is requested. You can change your choice later: where the rules require it, the game's Settings screen shows a "PRIVACY OPTIONS" button that reopens the form.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • The game only shows an ad when you tap "watch ad". It may load the next ad in the background in advance, once consent (where required) has been given.
  • DubIT Games still does not receive your name, email or other personal information from the game.

Purchases and subscription (Google Play)

  • Premium looks (a balloon or flame look, or a bundle of all four) are one-time purchases. Retries come in an optional pack of 5; each one continues a crashed run from its last checkpoint, and you can buy the pack again whenever you like. Double Coins is a monthly subscription that doubles the coins you bank when you clear a level. It renews every month until you cancel it, and you can cancel anytime in Google Play (Play Store > Profile > Payments and subscriptions > Subscriptions). Prices are shown in the game, as Google Play gives them for your country.
  • Google Play processes every payment. DubIT Games never sees or stores your card number, billing address or Google account details. Google Play tells the game only which products your account owns. Google's privacy policy applies to purchases.
  • The game uses Unity's In-App Purchasing package to talk to Google Play.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play under its own policy.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for or use your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera.
  • It does not use your precise location.
  • It does not contain analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services of its own that send data to DubIT Games. The ads service described above is the only one that receives data about your device.
  • It does not connect to the internet to play. It connects for ads and for Google Play purchases.

Permissions

The game uses these Android permissions.

  • Vibration, so that your device can buzz in time with the action.
  • Internet and network state, so that ads can load and Google Play can process purchases.
  • Google Play billing, so that purchases work.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID), which the ads service uses as described above. You can switch the advertising ID off in Android settings.

None of these gives the game access to your files, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.

Children

The game contains no personal-data collection by DubIT Games from anyone, including children. The ads service and Google Play handle data as described above. The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not designed for or directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given information through the game's ads or purchases, contact us and we will help you reach Google to have it removed.

Third parties

The game uses these third-party services that may receive data from your device: Google AdMob (ads, with Google's consent form) and Google Play (downloads, updates and payments, including Google Play Billing). It is built with the Unity game engine, including Unity's In-App Purchasing package.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date above changed. The version published at the same time as each game update is the one that applies to it.
